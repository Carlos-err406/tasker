Reminders on Android, with buttons to finish, snooze or postpone a task right from the notification.

## New

- **Reminders on Android.** Your phone now reminds you when a task is due, at its time or at 9:00 for tasks with only a date, just like the Mac. Reminders arrive even while Tasker is closed, and come back after the phone restarts. A task you've already completed, here or on another device after a sync, doesn't remind you. Tap a reminder to open its task. Android asks for permission to show notifications the first time there's something to remind you of, and you can turn reminders off under **Settings → Notifications**.
- **Done, Snooze 1h and Tomorrow on each reminder (Android).** **Done** completes the task (a repeating task moves to its next date), **Snooze 1h** shows the reminder again an hour later, and **Tomorrow** moves the task to tomorrow at the same time. They work without opening Tasker, and changes made while Tasker is closed sync the next time you open it.

## Install or update

**Android 11 or later:** download **tasker-android.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Later updates are available under **App options → View help → About Tasker**. Existing installs update in place and keep their data.

**macOS 13.5 or later:**

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Carlos-err406/tasker/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
```

The installer preserves tasks, backups and Google authorization. It supplies the tested SwiftBar fork when SwiftBar is missing; that fork is ad-hoc signed and may require macOS **Open Anyway**.

Google Drive features currently require an account enrolled as a test user in Tasker's Google project, and Google asks you to reconnect about once a week while the project is in testing. Offline use does not require Google. Open **Backups & Sync** on both devices, connect the same Google account, and turn on **Sync**. A safety snapshot is created before the first merge.

Downloads include the signed Android APK, the Mac runtime archive, their SHA-256 checksum files, and the Mac installer.
