Tasks can now repeat and have a time of day.

## New

- **Repeating tasks.** Add `*daily`, `*weekly`, `*monthly`, `*yearly`, or every N days, weeks or months (`*3d`, `*2w`, `*2m`) to a task's metadata line, next to its due date. Completing it moves it to its next date and back to pending instead of finishing it. A weekly show marked done late still lands on its usual day. **Won't Do** skips one date. Subtasks reset with their parent, so a checklist repeats too. Undo puts everything back. To stop repeating, remove the `*` token. Typing `*` suggests the common rules.
- **Due times.** Write a time right after the due date: `@sat 6:30pm`, `@nov1 9am` (24-hour like `18:30` works too). Tasks show the time next to the date, and repeating tasks show a repeat icon.
- **Agents** see each task's time and repeat rule, and completing a repeating task through the MCP server reports its next date.

```
Watch Frieren
@sat 6:30pm *weekly #anime
```

Reminders for due times come in a later release. Update Tasker on every device: a device still on 1.0 shows a metadata line containing a time or `*` token as plain text in the task, and doesn't repeat tasks.

## Install or update

**Android 11 or later:** download **tasker-android.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Later updates are available under **App options → View help → About Tasker**. Existing installs update in place and keep their data.

**macOS 13.5 or later:**

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Carlos-err406/tasker/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
```

The installer preserves tasks, backups and Google authorization. It supplies the tested SwiftBar fork when SwiftBar is missing; that fork is ad-hoc signed and may require macOS **Open Anyway**.

Google Drive features currently require an account enrolled as a test user in Tasker's Google project, and Google asks you to reconnect about once a week while the project is in testing. Offline use does not require Google. Open **Backups & Sync** on both devices, connect the same Google account, and turn on **Sync**. A safety snapshot is created before the first merge.

Downloads include the signed Android APK, the Mac runtime archive, their SHA-256 checksum files, and the Mac installer.
