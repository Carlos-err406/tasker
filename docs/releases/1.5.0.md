A home-screen widget for Android, priority and due date in the task menu, shortcuts on Tasker's icon, and tidier task rows.

## New

- **Home-screen widget (Android).** Long-press your home screen, open **Widgets** and add **Tasker list**. It shows a list's open tasks the way the app does: priority, notes, due date and time, repeats, links, tags and small previews of attached images. Tap the list name to show another list (or **All lists**), **+** to add a task to it, or a task to open it in Tasker. The widget updates whenever you leave Tasker.
- **Set Priority and Set Due Date in the task menu.** Right-click a task on the Mac, or swipe it or tap ⋯ on Android, to set its priority (High, Medium, Low or None) or due date (Today, Tomorrow, the coming Monday or Next week). **Remove due date** appears when the task has one. The task's metadata line is updated as if you had typed it, so its time, repeat, tags and links stay, and **Undo** puts back exactly what was there. The same options are on the menus of relationship lines.
- **Shortcuts on Tasker's icon (Android).** Long-press the Tasker icon on your home screen or app drawer and pick **New task** or **Search**. If you were in the middle of an edit, Tasker opens with your draft untouched instead.

## Fixed

- **Long words wrap inside the task.** A file path, link-like text or any long run of characters without spaces used to stretch past the task and make the whole list scroll sideways. It now wraps onto the next line, in the title and anywhere in the description: quotes, inline code, lists and tables. Code blocks still scroll sideways inside their own box.
- **No more gaps under a task's notes (Android).** Lines like "Subtask of …" and "Blocked by …" each left a band of empty space below them. They now sit right under the notes, and are just as easy to tap.

## Install or update

**Android 11 or later:** download **tasker-android.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Later updates are available under **App options → View help → About Tasker**. Existing installs update in place and keep their data.

**macOS 13.5 or later:**

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Carlos-err406/tasker/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
```

The installer preserves tasks, backups and Google authorization. It supplies the tested SwiftBar fork when SwiftBar is missing; that fork is ad-hoc signed and may require macOS **Open Anyway**.

Google Drive features currently require an account enrolled as a test user in Tasker's Google project, and Google asks you to reconnect about once a week while the project is in testing. Offline use does not require Google. Open **Backups & Sync** on both devices, connect the same Google account, and turn on **Sync**. A safety snapshot is created before the first merge.

Downloads include the signed Android APK, the Mac runtime archive, their SHA-256 checksum files, and the Mac installer.
