A Settings page, reminders on the Mac, and a date picker.

## New

- **Settings.** The gear in the Mac toolbar (App options → Settings on Android) opens this device's settings: **Show completed tasks** (now one setting per device rather than per list), **Media previews**, **Auto sort**, **Notifications**, and **Backups & sync**. Undo and Redo moved into the Mac toolbar.
- **Auto sort.** Keeps every list in system order after each change, including changes made by agents. Dragging to reorder is off while it's on.
- **Reminders on the Mac.** With Notifications on, Tasker reminds you at a task's due time, or at 9:00 for a task with only a date. Reminders missed while the Mac slept still arrive, up to 12 hours late. They appear as SwiftBar notifications, so allow SwiftBar under System Settings → Notifications. Android reminders come in a later release.
- **Date picker.** Type `@` in a task to pick Today, Tomorrow, Monday, Next week or any day from a calendar. Typing filters the list (`@we` → Wednesday) and previews other dates (`@+3d`, `@jan15`). After picking a date you can add a time; typing `6:3` suggests 6:30am and 6:30pm.

## Fixed

- Saving a task through the editor no longer doubles its blank lines. Blank lines a task already gained stay until you delete them.
- Mac settings such as media previews no longer reset when the Tasker service restarts.

## Install or update

**Android 11 or later:** download **tasker-android.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Later updates are available under **App options → View help → About Tasker**. Existing installs update in place and keep their data.

**macOS 13.5 or later:**

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Carlos-err406/tasker/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
```

The installer preserves tasks, backups and Google authorization. It supplies the tested SwiftBar fork when SwiftBar is missing; that fork is ad-hoc signed and may require macOS **Open Anyway**.

Google Drive features currently require an account enrolled as a test user in Tasker's Google project, and Google asks you to reconnect about once a week while the project is in testing. Offline use does not require Google. Open **Backups & Sync** on both devices, connect the same Google account, and turn on **Sync**. A safety snapshot is created before the first merge.

Downloads include the signed Android APK, the Mac runtime archive, their SHA-256 checksum files, and the Mac installer.
